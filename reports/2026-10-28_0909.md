## Laws of Malaysia (AGC)

### P.U. (A) 370/2026 – Income Tax (Deduction for Expenditure in relation to Cost of Renovation and Conversion of Office Premise to Residential House) Rules 2026

- **Section:** Principal Act / List of P.U. (A) / Finance Bill (latest legislation feed, tax-relevant only)
- **Publication date:** 27/10/2026
- **Type:** new
- **What changed:** New further deduction of 10% of the cost of renovating and converting an office premise into a residential house, incurred from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027, capped at RM10 million in total; effective from YA 2026. The office premise must have been used for administration/office management for more than 10 years and no longer be in use (shophouses excluded); the conversion plan must be approved by the local authority, the house used solely for habitation and rented out after conversion, and the costs certified by an external auditor. Not available if the same costs were claimed under s.33(1) or as capital allowances under Schedule 2 or 3. Made 24/09/2026.
- **Link:** https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputp/3793388/P.U.%20(A)%20370_2026.pdf

### P.U. (A) 371/2026 – Service Tax (Digital Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

- **Section:** Principal Act / List of P.U. (A) / Finance Bill (latest legislation feed, tax-relevant only)
- **Publication date:** 27/10/2026
- **Type:** amendment
- **What changed:** Substitutes regulation 16 (hours for submission of return and payment): returns and payment of service tax or penalty through electronic services can be received at any time except during system maintenance, using Malaysian standard time (UTC+8); where the last day falls on a Federal weekly or public holiday in Malaysia, or a weekly or public holiday in the foreign registered person's country, the deadline is that day. In force 28/10/2026; made 22/10/2026. Also published on MySST on 27/10/2026.
- **Replaces / supersedes:** Amends the Service Tax (Digital Services) Regulations 2019 [P.U. (A) 269/2019] by substituting regulation 16
- **Link:** https://pub-359af8e1f79c472292a7e44ec60f3027.r2.dev/SST%20Regulations/PUA%20371_2026.pdf
