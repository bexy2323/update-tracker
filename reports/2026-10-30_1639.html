Tax case report: MDCOGL v. Director General of Inland Revenue (SCIT, 21/10/2026) – interest income of a non-resident with a PE taxed at 24%, not 15%

  • Section: Legislation (everything)
  • Publication date: 21/10/2026 (decision); added to HASiL Tax Case page 30/10/2026
  • Type: new
  • What changed: The Special Commissioners of Income Tax dismissed the appeal of a non-resident company with a permanent establishment in Malaysia, which argued that its Malaysian-sourced interest for YAs 2016–2018 should be taxed at 15% under s.6(1)(b)(i) ITA 1967. The DGIR's position was upheld: the interest was connected to its Malaysian business, so it was taxable at the 24% corporate rate under s.6(1)(a), and the 15% rate only applies where the s.109 withholding mechanism applies. The additional assessments (Form JA) and penalties were confirmed; the taxpayer may appeal within 21 days.
  • Link: https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261021-REVENEWS-MDCOGL.pdf
