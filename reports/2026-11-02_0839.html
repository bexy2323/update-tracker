Technical Guidelines: Applications for DGIR approval under s.44(6) ITA 1967 for Houses of Worship Management Funds (Tabung Pengurusan Rumah Ibadat)

  • Section: Home > Kenyataan Media & Pengumuman (Media Statements & Announcements)
  • Publication date: 30/10/2026 (announcement); guideline dated 05/10/2026
  • Type: replacement
  • What changed: HASiL issued updated guidelines on how a house of worship management fund applies for approval under s.44(6) ITA 1967, so that donations to it are tax-deductible.
