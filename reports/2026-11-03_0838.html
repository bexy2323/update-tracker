MySST (Royal Malaysian Customs)

Service Tax Policy No. 6/2024 (Amendment No. 3) – Exemption from service tax on maintenance or repair services for MRO (maintenance, repair and overhaul) activities

  • Section: Pengumuman Terkini (Latest Announcements)
  • Publication date: 03/11/2026 (policy dated 02/11/2026)
  • Type: amendment
  • What changed: Adds a new category of exempt MRO service provider: Approved Maintenance Organisations approved by the Directorate General Technical Airworthiness (DGTA), exempt from 22 September 2026 to 31 December 2027. Existing exemptions continue: CAAM-approved AMOs and MITI/MIDA-approved shipbuilding/ship-repair companies (1 March 2024 – 31 December 2027) and foreign companies receiving MRO for foreign-going ships/aircraft (1 September 2025 – 31 December 2027). Eligible recipients are AOC airlines, ship owners registered with the Marine Department, Federal/State government departments and those foreign companies. Service tax already collected must be remitted, with no refunds for services before each effective date.
  • Replaces / supersedes: Cancels and replaces Service Tax Policy No. 6/2024 (Amendment No. 2)

Service Tax Policy No. 3/2026 (Amendment No. 1) – Service tax on maintenance charges and sinking fund for non-residential premises charged by developers, JMBs and MCs

  • Section: Pengumuman Terkini (Latest Announcements)
  • Publication date: 03/11/2026 (policy dated 02/11/2026)
  • Type: amendment
  • What changed: Maintenance charges and sinking fund contributions for non-residential premises charged to property owners by developers, Joint Management Bodies or Management Corporations (registered under the Strata Management Act 2013 or similar) are exempt from service tax from 1 July 2026 until the Service Tax Regulations 2018 are amended to make them non-taxable. There is no exemption before 1 July 2026: service tax due up to 30 June 2026 must still be remitted to RMCD. Taxable services that the developer/JMB/MC buys from third parties using these funds remain subject to service tax.
  • Replaces / supersedes: Cancels and replaces Service Tax Policy No. 3/2026
  • Link: https://pub-359af8e1f79c472292a7e44ec60f3027.r2.dev/Service%20Tax%20Policy/MS/DCP%203__2026%20(pindaan%201)%20MF%20%26%20SF_BM.pdf
