## Rahmat Lim & Partners (RDS) - Tax Focus

### RDS Tax Focus: SCIT allows tax deduction of intercompany back-charges under centralised management arrangements (M12 SB v DGIR)

- **Section:** Tax Focus publications (for the monthly update class)
- **Publication date:** 03/11/2026
- **Type:** new
- **What changed:** A group's management company paid site rentals, utilities and staff costs (salaries, bonuses, EPF, SOCSO) and back-charged them to the taxpayer, a ready-mixed concrete subcontractor. HASiL disallowed the deductions for YAs 2016–2018 because the tenancy agreements, bills and employment records were in other group companies' names, there was no written intercompany agreement, and it was concerned about double deductions. The SCIT allowed the appeal: deductibility under s.33(1) depends on whether the expense was incurred in producing the taxpayer's income and ultimately borne by it, not on who the named contracting party is. RDS stresses clear cost allocations, contemporaneous documents and consistent accounting, and recommends written intercompany agreements even though s.33(1) doesn't require them. A good candidate for the monthly update class.
