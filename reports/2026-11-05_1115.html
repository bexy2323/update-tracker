Infographic (Budget 2027 highlight): e-Invoice to be implemented by foreign e-commerce platforms – proposed effective date 1 January 2027

  • Section: Pautan Popular > E-Invois > Infographics
  • Publication date: Not stated (already on the infographics page when infographic review started, 05/11/2026)
  • Type: new
  • What changed: e-Invoice currently applies to local e-commerce platforms in Malaysia, which are responsible for issuing e-Invoices for all transactions made through the platform on behalf of sellers. To ensure equal legal treatment, the Government has agreed to extend this responsibility to foreign e-commerce platforms with transactions in Malaysia, whether with buyers in Malaysia or sellers from Malaysia (the infographic shows platforms such as Temu, Pinduoduo, Shein, Amazon, eBay, AliExpress). Proposed effective date: 1 January 2027; further details will be issued. Malay version only; HASiL has not published an English version.

Infographic: Examples of non-compliance and common mistakes to correct through the e-Invoice Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme (PKPS / SVDP)

  • Section: Pautan Popular > E-Invois > Infographics
  • Publication date: Not stated (already on the infographics page when infographic review started, 05/11/2026)
  • Type: new
  • What changed: Four examples HASiL expects taxpayers to correct under the e-Invoice SVDP: (1) not submitting any e-Invoices despite being mandated (e.g. mandatory from 1 July 2025 but still not implemented); (2) not issuing e-Invoices for all transaction types, e.g. only for operating income but not for asset disposals or rental income; (3) not following the prescribed treatment, e.g. issuing self-billed e-Invoices for transactions not listed in section 8.3 of the e-Invoice Specific Guideline; (4) not following the requirements, e.g. misusing the General TIN outside the situations allowed in Appendix 1 of the Specific Guideline. Further details are in section 17 of the e-Invoice Specific Guideline. Malay version only.
  • Link: https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/Info-e-Invois-Contoh-Ketidakpatuhan-Kesilapan-Lazim-Yang-Perlu-Diperbetulkan-Melalui-PKPS-e-Invois-BM.png

Infographic: e-Invoice date & time field can now be backdated for documents submitted under the e-Invoice SVDP (PKPS)

  • Section: Pautan Popular > E-Invois > Infographics
  • Publication date: Not stated (already on the infographics page when infographic review started, 05/11/2026)
  • Type: new
  • What changed: When submitting documents using the e-Invoice SVDP document versions (PKPS 1.2 or PKPS 1.3), taxpayers may now amend the e-Invoice date and time to an earlier date, such as the original date and time of the transaction. ERP and intermediary system providers must update their systems as set out in the MyInvois SDK (sdk.myinvois.hasil.gov.my > Release Notes > SDK 1.0 Release > Updates on e-Invoice SVDP). Malay version only.
