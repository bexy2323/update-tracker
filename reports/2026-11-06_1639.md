## HASiL (Inland Revenue Board)

### Tax case report: PT Travira Air v. DGIR (High Court KL, 02/11/2026) – helicopter payments to a non-resident taxed as rental of movable property (s.4A(iii)), not shipping/air transport income (s.54)

- **Section:** Legislation (everything)
- **Publication date:** 02/11/2026 (decision); added to HASiL Tax Case page 06/11/2026
- **Type:** new
- **What changed:** A non-resident company supplied helicopters (under a dry lease to Weststar) plus pilots and crew for offshore operations, with Carigali Hess paying it directly. The High Court upheld the SCIT: Weststar, not the taxpayer, provided the transport service, so the payments for use of the helicopters were income under s.4A(iii) read with s.109B (withholding tax on payments to non-residents), not air transport income under s.54. The assessments for YAs 2013–2015 stand; there was no double taxation because the 5% withholding tax already paid was credited against the 10% rate. The taxpayer may appeal to the Court of Appeal within 30 days.
- **Link:** https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261102-REVENEWS-PT-TRAVIRA-AIR.pdf
