Public Ruling No. 5/2026 – Taxation of a Resident Individual Part I: Gifts or Contributions and Allowable Deductions (8th edition)

  • Section: Legislation (everything)
  • Publication date: 04/11/2026
  • Type: replacement
  • What changed: Updated ruling on the gifts/contributions (s.44) and personal reliefs and deductions (ss.45A–51) available to resident individuals, now explaining the treatment from YA 2026 in line with the Finance Act 2024. Changes include updated information and examples across the reliefs; relief 6.16 is retitled to cover EV charging facility fees and the purchase of food waste composting and shredding machines for household use; and paragraph 6.22.3 adds new information. The ruling's own list of amendments is in paragraph 7.
