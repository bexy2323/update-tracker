MySST (Royal Malaysian Customs)

Guide on sales tax exemption under Item 58A, Schedule A, Sales Tax (Persons Exempted from Payment of Tax) Order 2018 – bunker fuel (Version 2.0)

  • Section: Pengumuman Terkini (Latest Announcements)
  • Publication date: 10/11/2026 (MySST announcement; guide dated 06/11/2026)
  • Type: replacement
  • What changed: Version 2.0 of RMCD's guide to the sales tax exemption for holders of a bunkering services permit (PDA 2) under the Petroleum Development Act 1974. Exempt goods: diesel, LNG, lubricating oil and greases (listed subheadings of 2710/2711) and B24 very low sulfur fuel oil (2710.20.00 00), bought from a registered manufacturer or brought from a special area, used solely as bunker fuel and delivered directly to eligible vessels: ships sailing from Malaysian ports to foreign ports, foreign ships on international voyages, international fishing boats, and ferries or cruise and tourist boats that call at foreign ports. Applications are manual (form at Appendix I, PDA 2 permit, CSA retail licence and the supplier's PO or proforma invoice) to the controlling customs station until MySST supports them; a CJ(P) Schedule A-58A report must be kept for every bunkering service, and sales tax becomes due if any condition is breached. Compared with version 1 (17/08/2021): B24 fuel oil added; CSA retail licence and PO/proforma invoice now required instead of the ship-stores approval letter; new notice to Customs before bunkering; the monthly CJ(P) report (due by the 10th) is replaced by a report per bunkering service kept for inspection. Malay version only.
