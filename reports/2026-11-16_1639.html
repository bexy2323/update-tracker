Form CP55 revised (Pin. 1/2026) – Authority to furnish prescribed forms/returns electronically (appointment of tax agent)

  • Section: Forms > Download Forms (years listed per form category)
  • Publication date: Not stated (new on HASiL Download Forms 16/11/2026)
  • Type: replacement
  • What changed: HASiL replaced Form CP55 with a 1/2026 revision. Taxpayers use it to appoint and authorise a tax agent and firm to file prescribed forms and returns electronically under the ITA 1967 (s.152), PITA 1967 (s.82), RPGT Act 1976 (s.57) and LBATA 1990 (s.21A). The new version identifies the taxpayer and the appointed firm by TIN. HASiL doesn't list the changes.
  • Replaces / supersedes: Form CP55 [Pin. 1/2019]
  • Previous document: Not available online: HASiL removed CP55_Pin2019.pdf (it now returns 404) and the Wayback Machine has no copy
