Infographic: MyInvois app now supports biometric login (fingerprint on Android/Huawei, Face ID on iOS)

  • Section: Pautan Popular > E-Invois > Infographics
  • Publication date: Not stated (new on the infographics page 18/11/2026)

Relief application forms updated: CP 15G and CP 136A revised to 1/2026; new CP 136H and CP 136J for petroleum income tax relief

  • Section: Forms > Download Forms (years listed per form category)
  • Publication date: Not stated (new on HASiL Download Forms 18/11/2026)
  • Type: replacement
  • What changed: HASiL refreshed its relief application forms. The forms requesting that a relief application be forwarded to the Special Commissioners of Income Tax are revised: CP 15G (ITA 1967, paras 97A(10)(a), 131(5)(a)/131A(5)(a)) and CP 136A (Petroleum (Income Tax) Act 1967) now at 1/2026. New forms CP 136H and CP 136J (2026) cover relief applications to the DGIR under the Petroleum (Income Tax) Act 1967 for an error or mistake (s.66(1)) and other than an error or mistake (s.66A(1)). CP 15C, CP 15F, CKHT 15H, CKHT 15J and CKHT 15K were re-uploaded at the same 1/2026 version. All are in Malay only.
  • Replaces / supersedes: CP 15G – 1/2022 and CP 136A – 1/2022
  • Previous document: https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/BORANG_RF_CP_15G_1_2022_1.pdf ; https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/BORANG_RF_CP_136A_1_2022_1.pdf
  • Link: https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/CP-15G.pdf
