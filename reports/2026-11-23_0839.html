HASiL (Inland Revenue Board)

Tax case: THMSB v. DGIR (SCIT, 13/11/2026) - expenses funded by an exempt government grant not deductible

  • Section: Legislation (everything)
  • Publication date: 20/11/2026 (date issued on HASiL's tax cases list; decision dated 13/11/2026)
  • Type: new
  • What changed: The Special Commissioners of Income Tax dismissed a property developer's appeal on a RM15.1 million Government facilitation-fund grant for PPA1M civil-servant housing, exempt under P.U.(A) 207/2006. Paragraph 3 of that Exemption Order disallows deductions for costs funded by the exempt grant. The developer had kept cost-of-sales deductions on RM3.55 million of grant-funded costs using a 'residual-amount' method and kept no separate grant account, so the additional assessments for YAs 2017-2019 (about RM1.36 million) and the 60% penalties under s.113(2) ITA 1967 were upheld. The taxpayer may appeal within 21 days of the decision.
  • Link: https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261113-REVENEWS-THMSB.pdf
