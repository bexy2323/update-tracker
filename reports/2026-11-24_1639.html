HASiL media release: tax refunds, e-Invoice figures, and Stamp Duty Self-Assessment Phase 2 from 1 January 2027

  • Section: Home > Kenyataan Media & Pengumuman (Media Statements & Announcements)
  • Publication date: 23/11/2026
  • Type: new
  • What changed: From HASiL's engagement session with chambers of commerce (Malay only). Phase 2 of the Stamp Duty Self-Assessment System (STSDS) starts on 1 January 2027, and there is no penalty throughout 2026 for submitting inaccurate stamp duty return forms (BNDS) under STSDS. The Stamp Duty Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme (PKPS), extended to 31 December 2026, had over 1.05 million applications with RM184 million of penalties fully remitted by 31/10/2026. Other figures: RM20.271 billion of tax refunds to 3.65 million cases by 31/10/2026, and 2.25 billion e-Invoices from over 270,000 taxpayers by 23/11/2026.
