HASiL media release: HASiL adopts SK Batu 38, Sabak Bernam under its MADANI Adopted School Programme

  • Section: Home > Kenyataan Media & Pengumuman (Media Statements & Announcements)
  • Publication date: 25/11/2026
  • Type: new
  • What changed: Not a tax update (community programme; Malay only). Under Program Sekolah Angkat MADANI (SAM) HASiL 2026, HASiL channelled RM100,000 to SK Batu 38, Sabak Bernam, Selangor, to upgrade the main hall and music room, provide sports equipment, a television and a PA system, and run teacher training. It also ran its Sahabat HASiL programme introducing over 60 pupils to the role of tax.
