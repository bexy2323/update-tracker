HASiL (Inland Revenue Board)

Tax clearance for companies (Forms CP7 and CP7(C)): Labuan entities must use Form CP7(LE)

  • Section: Forms > Download Forms (years listed per form category)
  • Publication date: Not stated (change first seen on HASiL Download Forms 27/11/2026)
  • Type: amendment
  • What changed: HASiL's notes on Form CP7 (Tax Clearance Letter for dissolution by strike-off) and Form CP7(C) (dissolution other than strike-off) now say that, while company applications must be submitted online via e-SPC in MyTax, Labuan entities (including those taxed under the Income Tax Act 1967) must apply using Form CP7(LE), and refer to Operational Guidelines No. 3/2026 on applications for Tax Clearance Letters. The sample CP7 form and Appendix 3A links were removed from the CP7 entry.
  • Replaces / supersedes: Earlier notes on the same page: CP7 / CP7(C) applications must be submitted online via e-SPC in MyTax (sample Form CP7 and Appendix 3A linked)
  • Previous document: https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/cp7_syarikat_2.pdf (sample Form CP7 previously linked)
  • Link: https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/muat-turun-borang/muat-turun-borang-lain-lain-borang/
