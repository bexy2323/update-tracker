## HASiL (Inland Revenue Board)

### Tax clearance (TCL) forms: CP7(LE) goes to HASiL Sabah's Labuan International Section; BE/BT/MT 2026 TCL forms must be filed with the YA 2025 form

- **Section:** Forms > Download Forms (years listed per form category)
- **Publication date:** Not stated (change first seen on HASiL Download Forms 27/11/2026)
- **Type:** amendment
- **What changed:** HASiL updated the notes on its Tax Clearance Letter forms. Form CP7(LE), for Labuan entities including those taxed under the Income Tax Act 1967, is now to be submitted to the Labuan International Section, HASiL Sabah (previously just 'submitted to LHDNM'). The Forms BE, BT and MT 2026 provided only for TCL applications adopt the YA 2025 format, so the 2026 TCL page must be submitted together with pages 1-4 of the YA 2025 form. CP7(C) now lists the cases it covers (company winding-up, cessation of business of a foreign company, amalgamation and merger, conversion to an LLP). The notes refer to Operational Guidelines No. 3/2026.
- **Replaces / supersedes:** Earlier notes on the same page (CP7(LE): 'can be downloaded and submitted to Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri Malaysia'; BE/BT/MT 2026 TCL forms listed without instructions)
- **Previous document:** Not applicable: the form files are unchanged; only the instructions on HASiL's Download Forms page changed
- **Link:** https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/muat-turun-borang/muat-turun-borang-lain-lain-borang/
