# Item 58A guide: version 1 (17 Aug 2021) vs version 2.0 (6 Nov 2026)

Guide on sales tax exemption under Item 58A, Schedule A, Sales Tax (Persons Exempted from Payment of Tax) Order 2018 (bunker fuel), RMCD. Both versions are in Malay only.

- Version 1: dated 17 August 2021, no version number. Copy supplied by the user (file "03-Panduan-Butiran-58A-Jadual-A_15jun2021.pdf"). Not available online: MySST replaced it at the same URL.
- Version 2.0: dated 6 November 2026. https://pub-359af8e1f79c472292a7e44ec60f3027.r2.dev/Specific%20Guides/MS/Panduan%20Pengecualian%20Cukai%20Jualan%20Butiran%2058A%20Jadual%20A.pdf

## Substantive changes

| Topic | Version 1 (2021) | Version 2.0 (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Exempt goods | Diesel fuel, LNG, lubricating oil, greases | Same, **plus B24 very low sulfur fuel oil (2710.20.00 00)** |
| Tariff subheadings | 2710.19.7200, 2711.11.0000, 2710.19.4300, 2710.19.4400, 2710.19.2000, 2710.19.3000, 2710.19.9000 | **2710.19.4300 removed; 2710.19.4500 and 2710.19.4600 added** (others unchanged) |
| Application documents | Form (Appendix I); PDA 2 permit; RMCD Customs Division approval letter to supply ship stores (must match the PDA 2 permit) | Form (Appendix I); PDA 2 permit; **CSA retail licence for scheduled controlled goods** (Control of Supplies Regulations 1974); **supplier's purchase order or proforma invoice**. The ship-stores approval letter is no longer listed |
| Controlling station | Based on business premises postcode | Same, now with a **postcode list by state (Appendix II)** |
| Manual applications | Manual | Manual **until applications through MySST are implemented** |
| Notice before bunkering | Not required | **New: notify by email/letter the Customs Division where the fuel will be obtained, before bunkering** |
| Report CJ(P) Schedule A-58A | **Monthly**, submitted **by the 10th of the following month** to the controlling CDN zone/state division | **For every bunkering service**, kept for records; submitted to the controlling station **only when a senior sales tax officer asks** |
| Purchase and use | From approved suppliers; used for bunkering in the approved area | From the **designated supplier**; used solely for bunkering in the **area stated in the PDA 2 permit** |
| Legal provisions cited | Amendment Order 2021 (in force 15 June 2021) | Sales Tax Act 2018 (s.35(1)) and the Amendment Order 2021 |

## Minor and form changes

- Eligible vessels unchanged; the examples of foreign ports ("Singapore, Thailand or Indonesia") were removed.
- Exemption certificate for goods from a special area: now shown to the proper customs officer.
- Condition on direct delivery reworded: v1 "can be proven that the goods will be delivered directly"; v2 "must be proven to have been delivered directly".
- Application form (Appendix I): approval number now "PDA 2 Approval No" only (was "PDA 2, RMCD etc"); goods table now asks for classification and description under the Customs Duties Order plus a description of the goods to be purchased; "bunkering activity" area renamed "bunkering service"; new "Part F: Applicant's declaration". The declaration now reads "transport/purchase raw materials" (v1: "purchase goods"). This looks like wording carried over from another form, but the guide does not say.
- Report form (now Appendix III, was Appendix II): no longer "monthly"; certificate validity shown as a from/to period; "Receiving port" is now "Next Port of Call"; opening/closing unsold balance columns replaced by one "Balance quantity in certificate" column.
- RMCD Internal Tax Division address moved from Menara Tulus, Presint 3 to Tower A, Suasana PjH, Presint 2, Putrajaya.
