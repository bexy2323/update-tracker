Afternoon team, a new RMCD guide is out: Version 2.0 of the Sales Tax Exemption Guide under Item 58A, Schedule A, Sales Tax (Persons Exempted from Payment of Tax) Order 2018. It's on the sales tax exemption for bunker fuel supplied by PDA 2 bunkering permit holders.

Key Points:
- Covers listed diesel, LNG, lubricating oil and greases, and B24 very low sulfur fuel oil, used solely as bunker fuel for eligible vessels (mainly ships on international voyages)
- Guide dated 6 November 2026 (Malay only); applications are still manual until MySST supports them
- Approved persons must notify Customs before bunkering and keep a CJ(P) Schedule A-58A report for every bunkering service
