Morning team, a new RMCD guide is out. It's on the sales tax exemption under Items 5, 6 and 7, Schedule B of the Sales Tax (Persons Exempted from Payment of Tax) Order 2018 for raw materials used to manufacture animal feed, fertiliser and pesticides.

Key Points:
- Covers import, transport and purchase of MOF-approved raw materials (listed with tariff codes), inserted by P.U.(A) 303/2026
- Effective date: 1 September 2026
- Certificate applied via MySST, valid 1 year; manufacturer needs a valid ML or MIDA exemption letter
- Report CJ(P) Jadual B-01 every 3 months; any breach makes the sales tax due and payable
